LOGO

The logo is a stylistic depiction of a solar eclipse.

SPECIFICS

The bounding box of the logo has side lengths of 11 units. The larger circle is tangent to the left and bottom sides of the bounding box and has a radius of 5 units. The smaller circle is tangent to the top and right sides of the bounding box and has a radius of 4 units.

The larger circle is colored RED. The smaller circle can be colored WHITE or BLACK depending on the background brightness.

The font used is Sofia Pro.
